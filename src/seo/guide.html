Practice 3BLD with BlindSolve

BlindSolve is a practice workspace for solving a 3×3 Rubik’s Cube blindfolded (3BLD). Work on memorization and execution separately, then bring them together in a timed solve.

Start with your lettering and method

In Settings, match the sticker letters, buffers and methods to the ones you use on your physical cube. The app includes M2 edges and Old Pochmann options. Check these settings before comparing a trace with your own memo.

Build a vocabulary you can recall

Use Letter Pairs to save memorable words for pairs of sticker letters. Choose words that are easy for you to picture. Then use Flashcards to practice recalling those words without a full solve.

Choose a focused drill

Training offers Memo Recall for remembering a memo, Trace Sprint for identifying targets, and Execution Sprint for practicing execution on a physical cube. Use a drill to isolate the part of a solve you want to improve.

Put the pieces together

Open the 3BLD timer, apply the scramble, then hold Space and release to start. Enable multiphase timing in Settings to separate memo and execution. After a solve, use memo reconstruction to review the target sequence and save the result in your session. Reconstruction is a review tool; it does not observe your physical turns.

You can practice as a guest. Keep your cube orientation consistent with your configured view and lettering.
